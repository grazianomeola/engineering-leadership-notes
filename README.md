# Engineering Leadership Notes

Written notes on how I lead engineering teams: owning delivery, handling production issues and keeping stakeholders aligned.

I'm **Graziano Meola**, an Engineering Manager with 30+ years in software, most of them in automotive embedded systems. For the last decade I have led an R&D software team at Eldor, now part of BorgWarner. I started as a C developer and moved through project leadership into engineering management. These days I own outcomes more than I write code, but I still read the plan, and the data, before I trust it.

I keep this repository because I believe in writing things down. A written decision can be reviewed, challenged and improved. A spoken one usually gets repeated.

## How I work, in short

- **Surface risk early.** Bad news gets cheaper the earlier it arrives.
- **Evidence over opinion.** Measurements, logs and bench data settle discussions that meetings don't.
- **Be honest about trade-offs**, including what we deliberately won't build.
- **Blameless, not toothless.** We look for what allowed a problem, then make sure the fix actually lands.
- **Put people where their strengths matter.** Many performance problems turn out to be placement problems.
- **Use AI tools daily**, for drafts, summaries and planning, and review anything technical or customer-facing myself.
