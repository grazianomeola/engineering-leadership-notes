# Lightweight automations: reusable AI prompts

My current automations are lightweight: reusable prompts I use with Microsoft Copilot to turn raw technical notes into clear written communication. I review anything technical or customer-facing before it goes out.

**Leverage:** a one-hour writing task becomes about ten minutes.

---

## 1. Customer status update

> You are writing a status update for an automotive OEM customer.
> Input: my raw technical notes below.
> Output: a short email in professional English with three sections: **Done since last update**, **Open points and risks** (each with owner and next step), **Next milestone**.
> Keep it factual. Do not add commitments or dates that are not in my notes. Flag anything ambiguous with [CHECK].
>
> Notes: ...

## 2. One-page executive view

> Turn the notes below into a one-page summary for senior management.
> Structure: **Situation** (2 lines), **Key decisions needed**, **Risks** (impact and mitigation), **Ask**.
> Plain language, no jargon, maximum 200 words. Do not invent numbers.
>
> Notes: ...

## 3. Slide-ready summary

> Convert the technical content below into bullet points for one slide.
> Title of at most 8 words, 4 to 5 bullets of at most 12 words each, and one line for the speaker notes.
> Keep technical terms accurate.
>
> Content: ...

## 4. Rewording a sensitive team message

> Rewrite the message below for my team.
> Tone: direct, respectful, no blame. Keep it short.
> Keep the factual request unchanged.
>
> Message: ...

---

*All examples are generic templates. No customer, project or company data is included.*
